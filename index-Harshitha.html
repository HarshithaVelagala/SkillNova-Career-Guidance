Policy Aligned

NEP 2020 & National Mandates

UniMonkeys isn't just innovative — it's exactly what India's education policies demand. We're built to fulfill NEP 2020's vision of experiential, vocational, and holistic learning.

NEP 2020 — Experiential Learning

The National Education Policy 2020 mandates experiential, hands-on learning from middle school. UniMonkeys is built to deliver exactly this — club-based exploration that goes beyond textbooks.

NEP 2020, Section 4.6 ↗

NEP 2020 — Vocational Exposure by Grade 6

NEP requires vocational skill exposure starting Grade 6 with bagless days. Our 6-club structure gives students structured vocational exploration every week.

NEP 2020, Section 16.5 ↗

CBSE Mandatory Club Activities

CBSE mandates clubs and life skills activities in schools. UniMonkeys provides a turnkey system that fulfills this requirement with real career-focused clubs, not just hobby groups.

CBSE Circular 2023 ↗
