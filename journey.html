THE JOURNEY
4-Year Elimination Pathway

A structured progression from exploration to mastery, guided by data-driven psychometric insights.

YEAR 1
Explore

Students sample all 6 clubs through mini-assessments and discovery sessions to find initial interests.

YEAR 2
Focus

Narrow down to 3 clubs based on psychometric scores, performance data, and personal preference.

YEAR 3
Specialize

Deep-dive into 2 clubs with advanced projects, mentorship, and competitive assessments.

YEAR 4
Master

Final specialization in 1 club with capstone projects, certifications, and career readiness reports.

Joining Late? No Problem!

Students joining after Grade 6 follow an accelerated elimination path with extra support.

Join in Grade 7

6 clubs → Grade 8: 3 clubs → Grade 9: 1 club

Students joining in Grade 7 start with all 6 clubs but follow an accelerated elimination path.

Join in Grade 8

6 clubs → Grade 9: 2 clubs → focus on 1

Late joiners explore all clubs intensively and narrow down quickly with extra mentor support.
